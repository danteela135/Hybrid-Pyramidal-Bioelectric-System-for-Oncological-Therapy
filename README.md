# Hybrid-Pyramidal-Bioelectric-System-for-Oncological-Therapy
This repository contains a public project and an open conceptual model exploring the application of bioelectric, optical, and geometric principles in supportive oncological therapy. The project is designed as an Open Source platform for idea exchange, further engineering modeling, and potential prototype development.
Content and Technological ConceptBiophysical FoundationsThe system is based on the theoretical differences in electrical potential and microenvironment between healthy cells and malignant cells:Constant Depolarization: Tumor cells are characterized by a drop in cellular voltage to -10 mV to -30 mV, in contrast to -60 mV to -90 mV found in healthy cells.Environmental Acidity: Due to altered metabolism known as the Warburg effect, the tumor microenvironment converts into a bolesna battery zone with low pH values between 6.2 and 6.5.Electrical Isolation: By closing gap junction proteins, the tumor tissue electrically isolates itself from healthy tissue control.System Architecture and Tetrahedron GeometryThe technical solution proposes utilizing a stable three-sided pyramid (tetrahedron) as a physical and geometric framework for wave manipulation:4 Radiation Sources: Emitters located at the four vertices of the tetrahedron send continuous waves into the tissue.Constructive Interference: Individual waves have a low, harmless intensity for healthy tissue. However, they meet in phase at the geometric center or focus of the pyramid, creating a localized high-energy sphere exactly at the tumor site.Spatial Positioning: By shifting phases via millisecond signal delays over a control chip matrix, the energy sphere can be moved within the body (up, down, left, right) without physically moving the pyramid itself.Optical Window of Human Tissue (830 nm to 860 nm)Therapeutic Window: Infrared wavelengths ranging from 830 to 860 nm (with maximum transmission between 800 and 875 nm) are minimally absorbed by blood and water, allowing deep penetration through bones and chest walls.Continuous Adaptation: Continuous Waves (CW) provide healthy cells with time to develop adaptive resistance and stabilize their mitochondria, whereas biologically sluggish tumor cells accumulate energy in the focus, leading to collapse.Detection Mechanism and 3D TriangulationSensors at 1/3 Height: Positioned at the centroids of the tetrahedron's lateral faces for mathematically optimal 3D coverage of the internal volume.Acid Refraction Reading: Sensors detect changes in wavelength and speed upon encountering the acidic medium and soft-map the boundaries of the tumor zone via fast three-dimensional triangulation.Electrochemical Feedback: Tissue impedance is monitored in real-time to automatically adjust the upper safety power limits based on the local pH factor.Peripheral Calibration and Immune ResponsePhantom Testing: Deploying a mini-measuring pyramid on the body's periphery (such as a thigh muscle) with a temporary contrast agent like Indocyanine Green (ICG) to define an absolute baseline for dosing.Immune Activation: The photoelectric effect on weakened membranes at the focus triggers the release of hidden tumor antigens into the bloodstream,
